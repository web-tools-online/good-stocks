Stock screener: US, EU & Czech stocks

Seven fundamental checks on every listed company, from revenue growth to PEG. The data refreshes every week.

Holdings: 0

A stock passes a criterion when its value meets the threshold. Missing data counts as a fail, except PEG: a stock without analysts' growth forecasts is scored without it (e.g. 6/6). Untick a criterion to ignore it. Your settings are saved in this browser.

How it works

Coverage

US: every common stock and ADR on NASDAQ, NYSE, NYSE American, NYSE Arca and Cboe, from the Nasdaq Trader symbol directory.

EU: stocks of EU-based companies on the main exchange of each EU country that Yahoo Finance covers: Xetra, Euronext (Paris, Amsterdam, Brussels, Lisbon, Dublin), Borsa Italiana, Madrid, Vienna, Nasdaq Nordic & Baltic, Warsaw, Athens, Budapest and Bucharest. A company listed on several of them appears once, on its home exchange.

Czech: all shares traded on the Prague Stock Exchange.

Metrics

Revenue Growth (1Y, TTM)
Revenue over the last 12 months compared with the 12 months before. * marks an estimate: the latest quarter against the same quarter a year earlier.
Earnings / Revenue Growth (4Y)
Change in net income or revenue across the last four fiscal years: the latest year compared with the oldest of the four (e.g. FY2022 → FY2025).
ROE
Net income (TTM) ÷ shareholders' equity.
Debt to Equity
Total debt ÷ shareholders' equity (most recent quarter).
Free Cash Flow (TTM)
Operating cash flow − capital expenditure, last 12 months.
PEG (5Y expected)
P/E ÷ analysts' expected yearly earnings growth over the next 5 years. Many European companies have no analyst forecast; they are scored without PEG (e.g. 6/6 instead of x/7).

Data & updates

A GitHub Actions workflow runs every Saturday. It collects the ticker lists, pulls the current fundamentals from Yahoo Finance and stores them in a SQLite database. Each run replaces the previous week's numbers. This page is then rebuilt from the database.

You can download the database and query it with any SQLite tool.

For information only, not investment advice. The data can be incomplete or wrong; always check a company's own filings.
